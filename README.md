# Business Radar

Business Radar is a deterministic business monitoring and decision-support app for
Frappe and ERPNext. It detects operational and financial risks, explains them with
evidence, and proposes actions for human approval.

Status: **Phase 1 (Foundation) in progress.** Not production-ready.

- Requires: Frappe v15, ERPNext v15.
- License: MIT (see `license.txt`).
- Documentation: `docs/`.

Design principles: ERPNext is the source of truth; AI is optional and never the
source of facts; every query is permission-aware; every action is whitelisted and audited.
